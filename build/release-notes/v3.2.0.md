## Download

- **MediaConverter-Setup.exe**: recommended. Installs for your account only, no administrator password needed. Installing over an older version keeps your settings.
- **MediaConverter-portable.zip**: no install. Extract anywhere and run `MediaConverter.exe`.

Check a download with `Get-FileHash .\MediaConverter-Setup.exe` against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, or with `gh attestation verify MediaConverter-Setup.exe --repo anglerequip/media-converter`.

Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the app isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
